Warrants (USDEW)

11.5M public warrants · $11.50 strike · 1:1 · expire Jun 25, 2031
What a warrant is
Each USDEW warrant is the right to buy 1 new USDE share from the company for $11.50, any time until Jun 25, 2031 — a five-year call option that StablecoinX itself wrote. With the stock below the strike, its entire price is time value: a bet the stock clears $— (strike + warrant price) before expiry.
  • Same thesis, more leverage: USDE only needs to re-rate to — of NAV by 2031 for warrant buyers to break even — the warrant is a geared bet on the discount closing, with a deadline.
  • Dilution if they pay off: full exercise mints 11.5M new shares (24.1M → 35.6M, holders own ~32% less per share) but delivers $132M cash to the treasury at $11.50/share. Above the strike, the honest per-share metric is fully-diluted NAV: $—/share vs $— undiluted.
  • The company holds a kill switch: if USDE holds above $10 for 20 of 30 trading days, StablecoinX can force-convert the warrants (capped at 0.361 shares each, or holders get $0.10); above $18 the redemption pays just $0.01. A sustained rally likely ends the warrants long before 2031.
