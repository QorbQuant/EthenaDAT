Reported holdings and share counts are forward-filled in the source dataset. This is a mathematical decomposition, not an explanation of investor motivation.

How to read this market

The mark price is Lighter’s valuation reference; it can differ from the last traded price and the Nasdaq stock price. The comparison uses the latest recorded USDE daily price, with its date shown. It is not a synchronized arbitrage spread or a prediction of the next stock opening.

Funding is the last settled hourly rate, not an estimate of the next payment. Positive rates mean longs pay shorts; negative rates mean shorts pay longs. Open interest is outstanding contracts multiplied by the mark price. It measures exposure, not a net long/short split. Missing data stays unavailable.

Source: Lighter market data ↗ · Funding methodology ↗. Market stats refresh approximately every minute while this panel is visible; hourly history refreshes every five minutes. Rate-limited requests back off and retain the last verified observations.

About StablecoinX

StablecoinX is a Nasdaq-listed company whose principal treasury asset in this dataset is ENA, the governance token of the Ethena protocol. This independent dashboard compares its share price with reported token holdings. The source series begins June 26, 2026.

Methodology & sources

Token NAV = reported ENA holdings × ENA price. Token NAV per share = token NAV / dataset Class A shares. mNAV = share price / token NAV per share. Indexed series are rebased to 100 on June 26, 2026. Holdings and share counts are forward-filled from reported events. Other balance-sheet assets and liabilities are outside token NAV.

Market observations: Yahoo Finance and CoinGecko via the repository. Company disclosures: SEC EDGAR. Options: Nasdaq. Daily series refresh from GitHub; market quotes are checked every minute. Quotes can be delayed. Token NAV excludes other assets and liabilities. Historical observations retain their recorded dates.

Methodology & definitions

Card spend sums successful USDe AllowanceSpent events emitted by user wallets on Avalanche C-Chain, filtered to the current and retired settlement addresses. Spend counts count individual log events, not distinct transaction hashes: one transaction can contain events from many wallets. The settlement-to-issuer transfer is excluded to avoid double-counting. Failed batch items emit no spend event. Median and average purchase amounts are omitted; these on-chain events are not a verified receipt-level purchase dataset. Deposits and withdrawals measure transfers into and out of card wallets, excluding purchases, refunds and rewards. Wallets created are deployed addresses, not verified individual users. Holding USDe is a snapshot balance test; ever spent is an all-time count. Daily spending wallets and rolling active-wallet counts use different windows and must not be summed.

Headline rolling windows follow the source query. The newest daily observation may be partial. Charts preserve observed dates; missing observations are not inserted as zero. Cumulative charts retain their all-time baseline when the visible range changes. The top-10 concentration figure is from the headline snapshot.

Sources & refresh information

Avalanche C-Chain data through Dune. The dashboard checks the repository feed every five minutes. Dune refreshes follow the repository pipeline; the update time above describes the source data, not your browser refresh. A notice appears when this feed is more than 36 hours old.
