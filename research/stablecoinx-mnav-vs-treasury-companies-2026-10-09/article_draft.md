# How does StablecoinX's mNAV compare with other crypto treasury companies?

Valuation review / Five crypto treasury companies at the October 9, 2026 close

By [Qorban Ferrell · @Degenerate\_DeFi](https://ethenadash.com/about/) · Published 15 October 2026 · Prices at the October 9, 2026 close · Filings to October 9, 2026 · Sources read October 12, 2026 UTC

At the close of trading on October 9, 2026, StablecoinX's stock, USDE, was priced at 0.46× the value of the ENA, the governance token of Ethena, that the company reported for each share. That was the second lowest of five crypto treasury companies, listed companies that hold one crypto token as their main treasury asset, whose filings gave counts that could be matched. Hyperion DeFi, which holds HYPE, the token of the Hyperliquid network, was lower at 0.34× on its common shares alone, a count that leaves out its convertible preferred stock. Forward Industries, which holds SOL, the token of the Solana network, was at 0.61×. Strategy and Strive, which both hold bitcoin, or BTC, were at 0.93× and 1.16×.

Ethena is the protocol that issues the USDe synthetic dollar, and USDE with a capital E is the stock. The multiple is mNAV, the share price divided by token NAV per share, as the [mNAV guide](https://ethenadash.com/research/stablecoinx-mnav/) explains. Token NAV, short for token net asset value, is the number of tokens a company reports times the token's market price, and token NAV per share divides it by the company's common shares. An mNAV below 1 means the stock market valued a company's common shares at less than its reported tokens, priced at the same moment. When debt or preferred stock has a claim ahead of the common shares, a figure below 1 does not by itself mean the shares traded below the company's net assets.

USDE closed at $12.30. ENA's last trade before 4 pm New York time on Kraken, a crypto exchange, was at $0.2112, which put the approximately 3.029 billion ENA that StablecoinX reported at $639.7 million, or $26.50 a share. The market value of its 24,139,375 Class A shares, the only class with a claim on dividends and assets, was $296.9 million at the close, $342.8 million less than the ENA. Put another way, USDE's close would have equaled token NAV per share at an ENA price of $0.0980, 53.6% below ENA's price at the close.

The measure counts each company's main token and nothing else. StablecoinX's cash and its liabilities were about equal at June 30, so leaving them out barely changes its figure. Counting Hyperion DeFi's preferred stock as the common shares it converts into raises its figure to 0.69×, and subtracting Strive's preferred stock and adding its cash and securities raises Strive's to 1.99×. BitMine Immersion Technologies, or BitMine, which holds ETH, the token of the Ethereum network, and Hyperliquid Strategies, which also holds HYPE, are left out because their latest filed token counts and share counts could not be matched.

## How the counts were chosen

Each count is the latest one the company had filed with the SEC by the close on October 9. StablecoinX's later filings rounded its ENA or listed the amounts received at the closing, so its count is the holding its closing release stated. A company is in the table only if its token count and its share count could be matched, with every change between their two dates reported in a filing. A search of the text of the filings of the seven companies other than Mega Matrix, from the dates of their counts to the close, found no later count than those used here, apart from StablecoinX's rounded figures, though three of BitMine's exhibits were images with no text to search. The eight companies checked are the ones whose filings this review read. Other crypto treasury companies that file with the SEC, among them SharpLink, Upexi and DeFi Development, were not read.

## Beyond token NAV

Token NAV counts one token and nothing else. It leaves out cash, other assets, debt and preferred stock. Its share count leaves out shares that options, warrants and convertible securities could add. Forward Industries publishes its own mNAV that counts some of these, so its figures differ from the table's.

Hyperion DeFi's 10-Q reported 5,235,897 shares of Series A preferred stock at June 30, each convertible into three common shares, with a liquidation preference of $50,740,000. Counted as the 15,707,691 common shares they convert into, they would raise its share count to 31,150,173 and its mNAV to 0.69×. Any of them converted between June 30 and September 30 would already be in the September 30 count and would be counted twice. Its filings from June 30 to the close included no prospectus and no 8-K item for an unregistered sale of equity, a change to holders' rights or a charter amendment. It also held $14.5 million of cash, cash equivalents and stablecoins at September 30.

StablecoinX had $18.9 million of cash and $18.3 million of liabilities at June 30, as the [book value review](https://ethenadash.com/research/stablecoinx-book-value/) lists them from the 10-Q. Adding the cash and subtracting the liabilities moves its mNAV from 0.4641 to 0.4637. Its share count also leaves out the 19,124,586 warrants and the 78,635 shares due on restricted stock units that its prospectus listed at August 28.

Strive reported no debt at September 30, and its balance sheet for that day gave its 12,940,621 SATA preferred shares a stated amount, the fixed dollar value assigned to each preferred share, of $1.29 billion, $100 a share. Strive's 13,498,082 SATA preferred shares on October 2 came to $1.35 billion at that $100. Subtracting that and adding its $284.7 million of cash and its $50.2 million of Strategy's STRC preferred stock leaves $13.95 a common share, and on that count Strive's mNAV is 1.99×.

Forward Industries published its own mNAV in the same release. Its net asset value added other digital assets and cash and subtracted $167.5 million of institutional debt, and at September 30 it put its mNAV at 0.684× on common shares and 0.946× on its fully diluted count of 105,535,801 shares, at $7.80 a share and SOL at $118.06. On the token-only count at the same prices it was 0.59×. With SOL at the October 9 close and its other lines held at their September 30 values, its own measure on common shares came to 0.71×. On the fully diluted count, the token-only figure at the October 9 close came to 0.84×.

Strategy reported $4.88 billion in what it calls its USD Reserve and $833.4 million in its USD Cash on October 4. It also had convertible notes outstanding at June 30, four series of its preferred stock traded on Nasdaq, and its proxy statement listed 7,750,000 shares of a fifth series, STRE, at September 15. This review did not total them, so it gives no net figure for Strategy.

The dashboard's own figure for StablecoinX on October 9 was 0.47×. It pairs each close with ENA's price at midnight UTC, 8 pm in New York the evening before, which was $0.2065 for October 9, as the [tracking review](https://ethenadash.com/research/usde-ena-tracking-2026-10-08/) explains.

## Caveats

The table is one close. StablecoinX's mNAV at the close ranged from 0.13× on July 24 to 0.52× on September 25 between June 26 and October 8, as the [tracking review](https://ethenadash.com/research/usde-ena-tracking-2026-10-08/) measured, so the order on another day can differ.

A low mNAV shows that the market valued the shares below the reported tokens. It does not show why. The five companies differ in size, in their debt and preferred stock, in the token they hold and in what they may do with it. StablecoinX could sell its ENA only with the Ethena Foundation's consent even after the lock-ups ended on October 5, as the [lock-up waiver review](https://ethenadash.com/research/stablecoinx-lock-up-waiver-2026-10-05/) explains. The comparison covers five companies whose counts could be matched and does not rank the sector.

## Reproduce the numbers

The main script checks every input against its recorded SHA-256 fingerprint, checks that all 69 quoted passages were recorded as found word for word in the filings, and stops before writing its tables if any of its 159 checks fails. The browser scripts read the filings and prices again and search the filings of the seven companies other than Mega Matrix for later counts. The downloads are the [five companies](/research-assets/stablecoinx-mnav-vs-treasury-companies-2026-10-09/companies.csv), the [alternative counts](/research-assets/stablecoinx-mnav-vs-treasury-companies-2026-10-09/variants.csv), the [companies left out](/research-assets/stablecoinx-mnav-vs-treasury-companies-2026-10-09/left_out.csv), the [chart data](/research-assets/stablecoinx-mnav-vs-treasury-companies-2026-10-09/chart_data.csv) and the [calculation files and source inputs](/research-assets/stablecoinx-mnav-vs-treasury-companies-2026-10-09/calculation-files.zip).

```text
token NAV per share  = tokens reported x token price at 20:00 UTC / common shares
mNAV                 = close / token NAV per share
implied token price  = close x common shares / tokens reported
Hyperion DeFi        = 3.40 / (83.38 x 1,850,000 / 15,442,482)
                     = 3.40 / 9.99 = 0.34
StablecoinX shares   = 24,029,375 + 5 x 22,000 = 24,139,375
StablecoinX          = 12.30 / (0.2112 x 3,029,000,000 / 24,139,375)
                     = 12.30 / 26.50 = 0.46
Forward Industries   = 7.34 / (108.38 x 8,501,298 / 76,288,215)
                     = 7.34 / 12.08 = 0.61
Strategy shares      = 402,337,658 + 19,640,250 + 92,894 = 422,070,802
Strategy             = 154.34 / (82,252.40 x 848,000 / 422,070,802)
                     = 154.34 / 165.26 = 0.93
Strive               = 27.80 / (82,252.40 x 29,462 / 100,938,986)
                     = 27.80 / 24.01 = 1.16
```
