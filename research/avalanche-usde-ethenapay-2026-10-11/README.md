# USDe on Avalanche and EthenaPay wallets, August 8, 2025 to October 11, 2026

These files reproduce every computed figure in the review of how much of the USDe on Avalanche C-Chain was in EthenaPay wallets at the end of October 11, 2026 UTC. USDe is Ethena's synthetic dollar, and EthenaPay is this site's name for Ethena Pay, Ethena's payment app and card. The review read every USDe Transfer record, the entry the token contract writes for each movement of USDe, on Avalanche C-Chain from the token's deployment on August 8, 2025 to the last block of October 11, 2026 UTC. A block is one of the numbered batches in which the chain records transactions. The review works out each account's balance at the end of every UTC day and splits the supply, all the USDe on Avalanche C-Chain, among EthenaPay wallets, the settlement and reward accounts and the holders outside EthenaPay. It also follows the USDe that moved between those groups, and ties the count to the token contract's totalSupply, the contract's own running total of USDe, and to the chain recount this site published with its EthenaPay adoption review, here the published recount.

No figure here is a forecast. Every number describes USDe recorded on Avalanche C-Chain by the end of October 11, 2026 UTC, apart from the totalSupply read on October 12 to tie the count to the contract.

## Reproduce

From this directory, with Python 3 and its standard library only. The scripts were run with Python 3.11 and 3.13.

    python3 avax_usde_review.py
    python3 make_chart_svg.py
    python3 check_article_numbers.py

The first script reads the inputs and runs its 74 checks. If any check fails it writes its log to `run_log_failed.txt` and stops before writing its tables, so `out/` keeps the tables of the last clean run. Otherwise it rewrites the tables in `out/`. It confirms every input against its size and SHA-256 fingerprint in `inputs/manifest.csv`, the chain files against `inputs/chain_manifest.csv` and the SHA-256 of `avax_usde_read.cjs`, and the chain read against itself, the token contract and the published recount. The supply on each day must equal all USDe minted less all USDe burned to the end of the day, and the five groups of holders in `daily.csv` must add up to it. The supply at the end of October 11 must equal totalSupply, read on October 12 between two blocks with no USDe minted or burned between them, less the USDe minted and plus the USDe burned after October 11 up to the later block. The USDe in wallets, the wallets created and the wallets holding USDe must match the published recount's `daily_chain.csv` on all 144 days from May 15 to October 5. The deposits, withdrawals, card payments, returns, rewards and transfers between wallets over those days must match its totals to the wei, the token's smallest unit. The script also checks the contracts the two wallet factories created, the addresses the dashboard's daily query leaves out by hand, and the three searches of the token's source. The second script draws the chart from `out/chart_daily.csv` and `out/results.json`. The third recomputes the article's computed figures, those in its metadata and the chart's text with separately written code, exits with an error if a phrase no longer appears, and checks the article's punctuation, names, links, addresses and closing line. None of them uses the network. `out/chart_preview.png` is the SVG on a page of 840 by 506 pixels, rendered at twice that size, 1680 by 1012 pixels, for link previews. A headless browser, a browser run by a script with no window, rendered it, and no script here writes it.

`avax_usde_read.cjs` produced the files in `inputs/chain/`. It reads the Routescan explorer API for Avalanche C-Chain. The Routescan explorer is a public website and API that indexes the chain. The script ran in a browser, on a page of the explorer API, as `AUR.run(u => fetch(u, { cache: 'no-store' }), log)`, from 12:52:13 to 12:55:31 UTC on October 12, 2026, and returned the files as text. It runs the same way with Node 18 or later as `node avax_usde_read.cjs`, which writes them to `inputs/chain/`. Its synthetic test runs with `node test_avax_usde_read.cjs avax_usde_read.cjs` and needs no network. A rerun gives the same `daily.csv` and `flows.csv`, unless the explorer's history of these blocks changes, and the same `holders.csv` unless a listed account's code changes, since the read checks for code at the latest block. Its `meta.json` differs in its run times and in the blocks between which it reads totalSupply, with the USDe minted and burned after October 11 up to the later block. Runs of earlier versions of the script, from 10:51:35, 11:09:34 and 11:16:06 UTC, wrote byte-identical `daily.csv` and `holders.csv` files, and the last of them the same `flows.csv` too.

The explorer answered a repeated request for the latest block, or for a call at the latest block, from a cache. Between 11:05:50 and 11:06:00 UTC on October 12 the plain request gave block 97,349,333 twice, 4 seconds apart, while the same request with an extra parameter gave 97,355,759 and then 97,355,763. The script adds the time as an extra parameter to the three requests that read the latest state, so that totalSupply and the blocks around it are read fresh. It waits 30 seconds before reading the mints and burns up to the later block, so that the explorer's log index has reached it. In the run used, totalSupply was read between blocks 97,362,127 and 97,362,128, and no USDe was minted or burned in block 97,362,128. The run of 10:51:35 UTC, before that change, tied the count to totalSupply at the cached block 97,349,333 instead, with the same result.

## Columns

Amounts ending in `_wei` are USDe in wei, 10^18 to one USDe. Amounts in `out/` are USDe rounded half up to the cent, each on its own, so a total can differ from the sum of its rounded parts by a cent. Shares in the article and in `out/` are worked out from the exact amounts in wei and then rounded, not from the rounded amounts.

`daily.csv`. `transfers` counts the Transfer records kept for the day. `minted_wei` and `burned_wei` are the USDe minted and burned, and `supply_wei` all USDe minted less all USDe burned to the end of the day. `minted_to_wallets_wei` and `burned_from_wallets_wei` are the parts minted straight into wallets and burned from them. The five columns from `wallets_held_wei` to `other_held_wei` split the supply at the end of the day among EthenaPay wallets, the settlement addresses, the reward accounts, the settlement recipients and the holders outside EthenaPay, the rhino.fi bridge contract and the Uniswap v3 pool among them. `holders` counts the accounts with USDe, `wallets_with_usde` the wallets among them, and `wallets_created` the wallets the two factories had created by the end of the day.

`holders.csv`. One row for each of the wallets, the settlement addresses and the reward accounts as a group, then the settlement recipients and the holders outside EthenaPay one by one by balance, the ten largest of the holders outside EthenaPay, with a row for the rest of each. `is_contract` is 1 if the account held code when the read checked it. `first_received` is the UTC day of its first USDe. `received_wei` and `sent_wei` count all USDe in and out, `minted_to_wei` the part minted straight to it, `received_from_settlement_wei` and `received_from_wallets_wei` the parts from a settlement address or a wallet, `burned_wei` the part it burned and `sent_to_wallets_wei` the part it sent to wallets.

`flows.csv`. `from_group` and `to_group` name the sender's and the recipient's group, with minted as the sender of a mint and burned as the recipient of a burn. `transfers` counts the records and `usde_wei` sums them. Transfers within a group appear with the same group on both sides.

`month_ends.csv` and `chart_daily.csv`. `ethenapay_wallets_usde`, `settlement_and_reward_accounts_usde` and `holders_outside_ethenapay_usde` are the chart's three bands and add up to `supply_usde` to within a cent. In `wallet_flows.csv` and `settlement_flows.csv`, arrived from another chain stands for minted and left Avalanche for burned.

## Names and terms

On Avalanche C-Chain USDe is the token at `0x5d3a1ff2b6bab83b63cd9ad0787074081a52ef34`. That is the address Ethena's key addresses page gave for most L2s, the layer-2 chains that settle to Ethereum, and the one rhino.fi's bridge configuration gave for USDe on Avalanche. The explorer named the token contract USDeOFT. Its verified source, the code its developers published and the explorer checked against the code on the chain, builds on LayerZero's OFT, or omnichain fungible token, contract. LayerZero is a protocol that carries messages between blockchains. In that source only `_credit` mints USDe, and only LayerZero's `_lzReceive`, which handles a message from another chain, calls it. Only `_debit` burns USDe, and only `send`, which sends tokens to another chain, calls it, through USDeOFT's own `_debit`. No other part of the source changes the supply. So a mint is USDe arriving on Avalanche C-Chain and a burn is USDe leaving it, and the downloads call them arrivals and departures.

A wallet is a smart-contract account, an account run by code on the chain, that one of EthenaPay's two factory contracts, `0x313be708df16979d9e5fe9db5ac4969b8add7207` and `0xac66ca9a79fb0d3c64cba44cd29610cd58602d6f`, created with CREATE2 on Avalanche C-Chain. CREATE2 is a way of creating a contract at an address fixed in advance, and this is the wallet rule of the published recount. The factories were created on May 15 and June 10, 2026, after block 85,400,000, where the read of the factories' internal transactions starts. The explorer listed 14,743 internal transactions for the two factories from that block to the end of October 11. Internal transactions are the explorer's records of calls and creations made by contracts. Every one of the 14,743 created a contract. 14,740 created wallets with CREATE2, and three used CREATE, one in the transaction that created the first factory and two in the transaction that created the second. The dashboard's daily query takes every contract the factories created and removes three addresses by hand instead. Two of them are the second factory's CREATE contracts. The third is a contract created without a factory by the account that created the second factory, 31 seconds before the second factory. The first factory's CREATE contract, `0x85d133fde5660220b0401600e113ae25ad9aebef`, is not on the query's list, which the adoption review gave as the likely cause of the one wallet the dashboard counted more than the published recount at its cutoff on October 5. None of the four contracts sent or received USDe to the end of October 11. The wallets created match the published recount on all 144 days to October 5.

The settlement addresses are `0x6070848bd19c37488d0516058f2933dd992733de`, in use, and `0x3b3ffd99b87a2dee5ad244b75d5a8e266890fdb8`, retired, the two addresses that received the USDe spent with the card. The reward accounts are `0xd0ec8cc7414f27ce85f8dece6b4a58225f273311`, which the dashboard's daily query labels yield, and `0xab2b06efa6179e624f5e3b08b64978df114ff24f` and `0xc9dd0d4351d3841a712f59cb4f1e88c3d500515b`, which it labels other rewards and whose purpose its notes call unconfirmed. A settlement recipient is an account that is not a wallet, a settlement address or a reward account and whose every USDe through October 11 came from a settlement address. Accounts tied to EthenaPay are the wallets, the settlement addresses, the settlement recipients and the reward accounts. The settlement and reward accounts are the same without the wallets, the chart's middle band.

The rhino.fi bridge contract is `0x5e023c31e1d3dcd08a1b3e8c96f6ef8aa8fcacd1`, the contract rhino.fi's bridge configuration gave for Avalanche. The explorer showed it as a proxy, a contract that runs another contract's code, here a contract named DVFDepositContract. The Uniswap v3 pool is `0x833e0ff43a8691f623cb8730ddb3e3180c7de08b`, which paired USDe with USDC, `0xb97ef9ef8734c71904d8002f8b6bc66dd9c48a6e`, at a fee of 0.05%. The holders outside EthenaPay are every account outside the groups tied to EthenaPay, the rhino.fi bridge contract and the Uniswap v3 pool among them, and the other accounts are the holders outside EthenaPay apart from those two. Wallets, settlement recipients and the other accounts appear as counts and ranks, never as addresses. The addresses in these files are protocol contracts and the accounts named in this section and in `meta.json`, apart from those in the dashboard's daily query and the web extracts, which keep their sources' text.

A card payment is a transfer of USDe from a wallet to a settlement address, the flow the cash-flow review calls card spend. Deposits are USDe into wallets from any account that is not a wallet, a settlement address or a reward account, minted USDe included, and withdrawals are USDe from wallets to any account that is not a wallet or a settlement address, burns included, as the published recount defines them. A return is USDe from a settlement address to a wallet, which the published recount calls a reversal. The published recount's spend events, the records logged for card purchases, numbered one fewer than the card payments to October 5, and they came to 5 USDe less, all of it on September 24.

A day runs from midnight to midnight UTC, and a balance or a share is the one at the end of the day. The explorer listed 63 Transfer records, all in August and September 2025, with an empty amount and an empty block hash, the identifier of the block that holds a record. The script read the receipt of each of their transactions, the chain's own record of what the transaction did, found none of the 63 there, and left them out. None of them involved a wallet, a settlement address or a reward account.

## How this review read its sources

The browser read the explorer API from its own page, as the published recount did. The files used came from the run from 12:52:13 to 12:55:31 UTC on October 12, 2026. The block times in `meta.json` place block 97,318,132 at 23:59:59 UTC on October 11 and the next block at 00:00:00 UTC on October 12. The browser read rhino.fi's bridge configuration at 10:50:34 UTC, Ethena's key addresses page at 10:50:52 UTC, the explorer's contract names and the Uniswap v3 pool's tokens from 11:02:26 to 11:02:30 UTC, the source of USDeOFT at 11:02:51 UTC, EthenaPay's FAQ at 11:11:58 UTC and its Terms of Use at 11:12:20 UTC. After a first independent review it read the factories' creation blocks from 12:06:55 to 12:07:01 UTC and searched USDeOFT's source again at 12:07:02 UTC. It searched the FAQ again at 12:07:32 UTC and the Terms of Use at 12:07:42 UTC, ignoring case, for rhino, bridge or bridging, LayerZero, cross-chain, crosschain and omnichain. After a second independent review it searched USDeOFT's source a third time at 12:52:14 UTC, read the creation of the three contracts the factories made with CREATE and of the third address the dashboard's daily query removes by hand from 12:58:17 to 12:58:47 UTC, and the USDe Transfer records of those four contracts from 13:00:01 to 13:00:12 UTC, with a control query on the rhino.fi bridge contract at 13:00:50 UTC, all on October 12. The published recount's daily file and the dashboard's daily query came from the site's repository at commit 8492959, copied at 11:00:53 UTC on October 12. The browser downloaded nothing to disk.

## Limits

A wallet is not a person, and one person may hold several wallets. The chain does not show who controls the settlement recipients, the other accounts or the rhino.fi bridge contract, or whether EthenaPay's app routes deposits through rhino.fi, and the FAQ and Terms of Use read on October 12 did not name rhino.fi or any bridge. The settlement recipients count as tied to EthenaPay only because all their USDe came from the settlement addresses. The shares are for the end of each UTC day and move from day to day. The count covers Avalanche C-Chain alone. The wallets' balances and flows from May 15 to October 5 match the published recount, while those of October 6 to 11 rest on this read alone. The supply on October 11 ties to totalSupply, but no outside source checks how the USDe outside the wallets splits among the other groups.
